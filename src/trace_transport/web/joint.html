What is being trained

Text/status messages share the connection with encoded image buffers and larger map deltas. Producers slow down when their queues fill; admitted messages are never silently discarded. Wi-Fi conditions persist, degrade, and recover.

The simulator uses byte buffers sized like compressed frames and map updates. It does not decode images, control a robot, or model full 802.11 radio behavior.
